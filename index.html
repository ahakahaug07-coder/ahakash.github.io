§ 01 — About

A practitioner becoming an engineer of supply chains.

I started in the operations room — handling on-board courier shipments, calculating quotes under deadline pressure, and untangling customs clearance for clients who could not afford a delay. That experience taught me that supply chains are won at the seams: the moment a quote is sent, the hour a document is filed, the row in a spreadsheet that nobody updated.

At IMT Atlantique I developed that operational instinct through optimisation, simulation, forecasting and data analysis in R and Python. I completed my master's thesis in October 2026 on selecting airline carriers to reduce freight costs while considering operational risk.

Today my focus sits at the intersection of logistics, analytics, and automation. Building things that take the boring work off humans so they can spend their time on what's actually hard.
